Privacy

Updated 2026-11-22

Home page

The home page counts visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no third-party cookies. Visit statistics can include the page address, the site you came from, your approximate location, and browser and device information. Vercel tells visitors apart with a hash of the request that it discards after 24 hours. We see totals, not who you are.

The free tools

The x402 setup check and the record verifier check your files in your browser. They do not upload your files or records, and neither tool includes the visit-counting script.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard request logs to run and protect the service. These can include the page requested, the time and your IP address.

Email

If you write to hello@counterroll.dev, we keep your message and address to reply to you.
